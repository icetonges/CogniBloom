## Ask the teacher (5)

These need a decision before buying or borrowing — the wrong edition means the wrong page numbers, or in two cases a different book entirely.

- **#14 The Diary of Anne Frank** — A widely staged 1997 revision by Wendy Kesselman also exists. Ask which script the class uses.
- **#28 Jim Thorpe, Original All-American** — A fictionalized biography told in Thorpe’s first-person voice — some catalogs shelve it as a novel.
- **#29 I Am Malala: How One Girl Stood Up for Education and Changed the World** — TWO editions exist. The adult original (2013) is co-written with Christina Lamb; the Young Readers Edition (2014) with Patricia McCormick. A grade-7 list almost certainly means the Young Readers Edition — listed here — but confirm before buying.
- **#40 March: Book One** — A trilogy — Book One (2013), Two (2015), Three (2016). Confirm which volume(s) the class assigns.
- **#42 The Children of Willesden Lane: Beyond the Kindertransport — A Memoir of Music, Love, and Survival** — A young-readers adaptation (2017) also exists. Confirm which edition is assigned.
